BotDisc

BotDisc is a personal backup tool used only by its owner (lexfin67@gmail.com). It uploads encrypted backups of the owner's private server to the owner's own Google Drive.

Privacy Policy

BotDisc does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data of third parties. It accesses only files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope). No analytics, no advertising.
